# AI disclosure

This research project was developed in an AI-assisted open-mathematics
workflow under human direction. Multiple model instances proposed independent
approaches; separate adversarial lanes then examined the mathematical chain,
source scope, and priority. Codex reconciled local repairs, maintained the
claim boundary, and assembled this sanitized repository.

The current audits are AI-based and are not represented as human peer review.
A model verdict is not itself evidence: the public-safe record distinguishes
the exact proof text, mathematical audit, literature search, integrity checks,
and any future proof-assistant artifact.

No model is listed as a human author. `CITATION.cff` records the owner's
established public convention, `DannyExperiments`, as repository authorship.
Private chat URLs, raw receipts, personal screenshots, and local absolute
paths are intentionally excluded from this public tree.
