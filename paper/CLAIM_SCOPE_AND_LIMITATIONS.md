# Claim scope and limitations

## New theorem proved in this manuscript

For each fixed `p in (1/2,1)`, the expected-distance exponent in the random
series--parallel graph is characterized as the attained principal eigenvalue
of the explicit decreasing-quantile operator `T_p`. The manuscript proves the
lower and upper Collatz--Wielandt formulas and a compatible invariant-measure
variational formula.

## Endpoint attribution

- `p=1/2`: the identity `delta(1/2)=0` is prior work of Chen, Derrida,
  Duquesne, and Shi. It is cited and is not claimed as a new theorem here.
- `p=1`: `Delta_n=2^n`, so `delta(1)=log 2` elementarily.
- The new interior theorem plus those two endpoint facts yields a complete
  characterization on `[1/2,1]` in the sense of Benjamini's Question 9.6.

## Claims deliberately excluded

- no elementary scalar formula for `delta(p)`;
- no uniqueness of the eigenprofile;
- no convergence theorem for every normalized orbit;
- no statement that every maximizing invariant measure is a fixed-point mass;
- no compactness theorem at `p=1/2`;
- no absolute historical-priority claim;
- no peer-review or proof-assistant-verification claim.

## Priority language

A documented search current through 2026-08-09 found no public work stating
the same all-interior, model-specific eigenprofile/Collatz--Wielandt or
invariant-measure characterization. The recursion and abstract machinery are
prior art. This is a qualified negative-search result, not proof of absolute
priority.
