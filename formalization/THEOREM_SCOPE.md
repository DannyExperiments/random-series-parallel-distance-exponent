# Formalization theorem scope

The formalization request does not target the full spectral characterization.
It targets a partial logical core supporting the repaired proof.

Success means a pinned Lean project proves the finite-tree concavity/Jensen
block lemma and normalized second-moment algebra without `sorry`, `admit`,
unsafe shortcuts, custom axioms, or theorem-shaped placeholders.

Success does not establish:

- existence or continuity of the quantile operator;
- compactness of \(K_p\);
- the Schauder fixed point;
- existence or maximality of the eigenprofile;
- either Collatz--Wielandt formula;
- the invariant-measure formula;
- the residual interior problem.
