receipts, URLs, and source-search logs remain in the permanently private
evidence case.

- `public_safe_reports/MATHEMATICAL_AUDIT_STATUS.md`: repair and re-audit gate.
- `public_safe_reports/PRIORITY_AUDIT_STATUS.md`: source and novelty gate.
- `public_safe_reports/PRIORITY_AUDIT_EXACT_RESULT_2026-08-09.md`: fresh
  exact-result and collision search.
- `public_safe_reports/PRIORITY_AUDIT_ARCHITECTURE_2026-08-09.md`: independent
  architecture and terminology search.
